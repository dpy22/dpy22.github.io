## Bringing equipment into one operating problem

Changing renewable generation calls for more flexible coordination between generation and storage. An object-oriented approach represents boilers, gas turbines, batteries, and heat accumulators, considering equipment state alongside system objectives.

## From optimisation to a software interface

The approach uses two-level particle swarm optimisation, accounting for AGC instructions, wind generation, device state, and economics. As a co-first contributor, I worked on algorithms and the front-end interface to express models and operating strategies through software.

## A collaborative outcome

The project received Second Prize in the 42nd Tsinghua Challenge Cup and the 18th Energy & Power Design Competition, and registered software copyright. It was a collaborative competition and software project.
